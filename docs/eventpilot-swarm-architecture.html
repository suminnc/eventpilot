What actually makes them check each other

  1. Decision rights, not vibes. Exec = scope and over-budget approval; Logistics lead = what's needed and which vendor; Finance = affordability veto; sub-agents = options and trade-offs.
  2. Bounded disagreement. Quality-vs-cost survives at most 2 negotiation rounds, then it escalates to an Exec decision memo with the cost delta and experience impact.
  3. A real veto. Finance marks each item APPROVED / APPROVED WITH CONDITION / VETOED, and reconcile must resolve every veto before anything proceeds.
  4. Adversarial verification. A separate verifier that wrote none of the deliverables checks ownership, dates against the T-minus gates, unsigned spend counted as committed, and unsourced numbers.
  5. Hard guardrails. 10–15% contingency, deposits ≤ 50%, no spend without a signed quote/contract, >5% variance needs Exec sign-off.
